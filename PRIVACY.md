# StudyPath privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

StudyPath is a plugin for Claude. It consists of text instructions (skills and commands) and a list of third-party connectors. It has no server of its own.

## What StudyPath collects

Nothing. The plugin does not operate any server, database, analytics or logging. The plugin author receives no data from your use of it, other than the aggregate install and usage figures that Anthropic shows to every plugin publisher in its developer portal.

## What StudyPath sends, and where

When you use the plugin, Claude sends your requests to the connectors you have chosen to connect, and only when a task needs them:

- Coursera, Udemy Business, O'Reilly: your search terms and the lessons you ask about.
- Kuliko: the flashcards you confirm.
- Google Calendar: a read of the time window you name, to find free slots, and the events you confirm.
- Notion: the plan page you confirm.

Each connector is operated by its own company under its own privacy policy and your own account with that company. Connect only the ones you want; the plugin works without any of them.

## What StudyPath does not do

- It does not read your email, your other calendar events, or your other Notion pages.
- It does not edit or delete anything that already exists in those services.
- It does not use Claude's memory or your past chats.
- It does not store anything between conversations.

## Children

StudyPath is not intended for people under 18.
